Biography

Keyan Wang

Doctoral student in Oncology
Peking University Cancer Hospital

I completed my undergraduate medical education at Jilin University in 2026 and am pursuing doctoral study in oncology at Peking University Cancer Hospital.

My research experience includes experimental work on glioma stem cell differentiation and reviews of therapeutic delivery systems and photobiomodulation. These projects have shaped my interest in connecting disease mechanisms with treatment opportunities.

My current research focuses on precision oncology, particularly gastrointestinal cancer. I am also developing skills in computational biology, including the analysis of bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomic data.

I value research that combines a clear biological question, careful analysis, and experimental or clinical relevance.
