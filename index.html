About

From medical training
to translational research.

I am a doctoral student in oncology at Peking University Cancer Hospital and a 2026 medical graduate of Jilin University. I am interested in how biological mechanisms can inform more precise approaches to cancer treatment.

My published work spans glioma stem cell differentiation, nanomedicine for glioblastoma, and photobiomodulation in diabetes. My current interests include gastrointestinal cancer, the tumour microenvironment, and cancer multi-omics.
