Research project · Manuscript in preparation

MTAP loss.
From RNA to cellular context.

A pan-cancer investigation of MTAP deletion, microenvironmental remodelling, and potential therapeutic vulnerabilities.

Scientific question

How does MTAP loss relate to the tumour ecosystem?

This project explores associations between MTAP deletion, cancer cell states, and the immune and stromal microenvironment. RNA-based prediction provides an approach to studying public single-cell cohorts that lack matched genomic labels.

The analysis combines bulk model development with patient-level single-nucleus and single-cell pseudobulk evaluation. Functional and microenvironmental analyses form part of the ongoing research.

Prediction workflow

Define the cohort

Audit primary tumour RNA and DNA inputs and define genomic outcome labels.

Develop models

Use a fixed patient split and training-only preprocessing to compare three approaches.

Evaluate transfer

Assess the holdout, external bulk cohorts, and patient-level pseudobulk settings.

Study cell states

Explore cancer and microenvironmental phenotypes associated with predicted status.

Predictions are evaluated at the patient level. A predicted label does not establish the genomic status of an individual cell. The manuscript is in preparation, and the repository records the current analysis and reproducibility status.

Code & teaching

An open research
and learning resource.

The model workflow also serves as a Python programming course project. Its nine sections cover data ingestion, cohort definition, preprocessing, model development, independent evaluation, transfer, and visualization.

The public repository contains code and documentation. Expression matrices, patient-level records, author-provided mappings, and model checkpoints are not redistributed.
