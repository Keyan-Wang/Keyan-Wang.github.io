Research interests

Mechanisms, treatments,
and patient context.

My interests span cancer cell biology, therapeutic strategies, and computational approaches to precision oncology.

Cancer cell states & differentiation

My experimental research has examined glioma stem cell differentiation, with a focus on taxifolin, CYP1B1, and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition. I am interested in how cellular plasticity and stem-like states relate to treatment response.

Current interests extend to the relationships between genomic alterations, malignant cell states, and the immune and stromal microenvironment.

Therapeutic mechanisms & delivery

My published reviews cover nano-delivery strategies for glioblastoma and photobiomodulation in diabetes and its complications. These topics share a focus on biological mechanisms, therapeutic barriers, and the interpretation of preclinical and clinical evidence.

In oncology, I am particularly interested in the biological conditions required for targeted therapies and immunotherapies to be effective.

NanomedicineTherapeutic deliveryMechanistic evidence

Precision oncology & multi-omics

My current work uses public genomic and transcriptomic resources to study cancer phenotypes at patient and cellular resolution. Interests include gastrointestinal cancer, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, and interpretable machine learning.

The ongoing MTAP project is one application of these approaches. Its code documents model development, independent evaluation, and patient-level pseudobulk transfer.
